Accessory Styling Tips & Tricks

Elevate your outfits with the perfect accessories

The Power of Accessories

Accessories can completely transform an outfit. They're the easiest way to express personality and style.

Jewelry Styling Basics

  • Match metals: Mix gold or silver, not both
  • Balance: If statement necklace, wear simple earrings
  • Stack rings for modern look
  • Layer necklaces for dimension

Statement Jewelry

Bold pieces draw attention. One statement piece per outfit works best. Choose either a statement necklace OR earrings, not both.

Bags Matter

  • Neutral bags match with everything
  • Bag color should complement your outfit
  • Size should be proportional to your frame
  • Consider the occasion

Scarf Styling

Scarves add color, pattern, and sophistication:

  • Tie around neck
  • Wrap as a belt
  • Drape over shoulder
  • Use as a headscarf

Shoe Selection

Shoes complete the outfit:

  • Heel height affects silhouette
  • Shoe color affects overall proportion
  • Comfort is non-negotiable
  • Match formality level to occasion

Hair & Makeup as Accessories

These complete your accessory ensemble. Balance jewelry with simple hairstyles or vice versa.

Styling Complete Custom Looks

Our custom designs are created with accessory styling in mind. We design pieces that work beautifully with simple accessories or shine as statement pieces themselves. Consult with our stylists to create the perfect complete look.
