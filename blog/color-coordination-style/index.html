Color Coordination & Matching Guide

Master the art of creating beautiful color combinations

Understanding Color Theory

Colors work together following specific principles. Understanding these creates harmonious outfits.

Complementary Colors

Colors opposite on the color wheel create vibrant contrast. Examples: blue and orange, purple and yellow.

Analogous Colors

Colors next to each other create harmony. Example: blue, blue-green, and green.

Warm vs. Cool Tones

Understanding whether you're warm or cool-toned helps select colors that flatter:

  • Warm tones: Gold, warm greens, oranges, warm reds
  • Cool tones: Silver, cool blues, cool purples, cool pinks

Skin Tone Color Matching

Your skin tone determines which colors make you glow:

  • Fair skin: Jewel tones, pastels, bold colors
  • Medium skin: Warm or cool tones, earth tones
  • Deep skin: Jewel tones, warm colors, bright colors

The 60-30-10 Rule

A balanced outfit uses:

  • 60% dominant color (base)
  • 30% secondary color (accent)
  • 10% pop of color (standout piece)

Neutral Combinations

Safe, sophisticated color combinations: black & white, navy & cream, gray & white.
