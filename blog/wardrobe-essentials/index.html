Must-Have Wardrobe Essentials

Build a foundation for endless outfit combinations

The Foundation of Style

A well-curated wardrobe is built on timeless essentials that mix and match effortlessly.

1. Classic White Button-Up Shirt

Versatile, professional, and endlessly styleable. Wear it tucked, open, or oversized.

2. Well-Fitted Jeans

Invest in quality denim. Dark wash is most versatile.

3. Neutral Trousers

Black, navy, or tan trousers in well-fitting cuts for professional settings.

4. Black Blazer

The ultimate power piece that elevates any outfit instantly.

5. Plain T-Shirts

Quality basics in black, white, and neutral tones in various styles.

6. Simple Sweater

A classic crew neck or v-neck in a neutral color for layering.

7. Neutral Cardigan

Perfect for layering and adding warmth without bulk.

8. Black Dress

The little black dress. Timeless and goes anywhere.

9. Neutral Bag

An everyday bag in black, brown, or tan that matches everything.

10. Classic Shoes

Black flats, white sneakers, neutral heels, and loafers for versatility.

Elevate Your Essentials

Once you have these essentials, elevate them with custom pieces from Miss Khan Designs. Custom styling adds personality and uniqueness to your basic wardrobe. Let's create custom pieces that reflect your style.
